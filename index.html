Four moves

Essay mode uses your saved study artworks. Save a work first to make the questions personal.

  1. ContextWho made it, when, where, and for what world?
  2. Visual evidenceWhat do line, color, body, space, surface, or scale do?
  3. ArgumentWhat claim are you making, not just what facts do you know?
  4. ImplicationWhat changes when we understand the work this way?
